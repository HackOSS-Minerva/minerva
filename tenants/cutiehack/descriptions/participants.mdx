# Cutie Hack 2026 - Participant Registration

Welcome to Cutie Hack 2026! Thank you for considering becoming a participant, we appreciate your enthusiasm to join us this year. Cutie Hack is UC Riverside’s 12 hour, beginner-friendly hackathon hosted by the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) at UCR.

## **Event Information**

**Cutie Hack 2026** takes place throughout **Saturday, November 21, 2026** at **UC Riverside - Winston Chung**.

Participants are not required to be in person for the full duration, but are welcome to the following benefits:

- Connect with other participants, industry professionals, and sponsors
- Can attend any of our technical workshops, industry panel, and activities
- Free swag (first come, first serve), including shirts, stickers, and more

For participants that intend to submit a project: judging will be in person only and the majority of your team must be present.

Note: participants are not permitted to become judges for the hackathon.
