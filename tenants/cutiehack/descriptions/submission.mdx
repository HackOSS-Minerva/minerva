# Cutie Hack 2026 - Project Submission
Submit your project for judging by the time hacking ends on **November 21, 2026**!

Ensure to fill out your team information and provide the required links to your deliverables:
- Invite your teammates (if applicable) to this submission using their email addresses!
- **You must submit** your team name, project name, Devpost submission link, and GitHub repository
- Make sure that all the naming conventions are consistent (e.g. your team and project name must match your Devpost project name)

On your Devpost submission (see our [page](https://cutie-hack-2026.devpost.com)):
- **Required**:
  - Ensure all team members are invited to your Devpost submission link
  - GitHub repository link to your project’s codebase
  - Description of your project, what it does, and what problems it solves
  - Indicate the top 3 track(s) your team is aiming/eligible for (1st, 2nd, 3rd Place do not count)
- Optional, but helpful:
  - Live demo/website link (if available)
  - Video demo
  - Figma design file
