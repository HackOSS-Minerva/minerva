# Cutie Hack 2026 - Judge Registration

Welcome to Cutie Hack 2026! Thank you for considering becoming a judge, we appreciate your efforts to help support us this year. Cutie Hack is UC Riverside’s 12 hour, beginner-friendly hackathon hosted by the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) at UCR.

## Event Information

**Cutie Hack 2026** takes place throughout **Saturday, November 21, 2026** at **UC Riverside - Winston Chung**.

Judge responsibilities include:

- Participate in judging and judging debrief on the day of the hackathon (will take place **in person only**)
- **Evaluate hackathon projects** based on judging criteria (idea, value, technical complexity/elements, feasibility, scalability)
- After reviewing projects, judges will collectively **determine the winners** for each track prize category

Judges are not required to stay the full duration of the event, but are more than welcome to grab free swag (first come, first serve), meals, mini events (workshops/activities), and additional opportunities throughout the day.

Note: judges are not permitted to become participants for the hackathon.
