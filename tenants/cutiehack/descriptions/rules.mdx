# Rules
## Guidelines
**Respect:** Be respectful of your peers and any attendee of the hackathon! If there is a situation that you would like to report, fill out our form: (google form link).
**Teams:** Teams can be 1-4 participants.
**Coding:** Ideation before the event is allowed, but all coding/building must take place during the time frame provided on the day of the hackathon (previous projects may not be submitted).
**Project Submission:** All projects must be submitted to Devpost by the deadline (further requirements can be found on our [Devpost page](https://cutie-hack-2026.devpost.com)).
**Judging:** Teams that plan to submit a project are required to be in person (UC Riverside - Winston Chung) for the duration of judging.

## Eligibility
- Cutie Hack is open to current college students (undergraduate, graduate, etc.) and recent graduates (within the past 12 months)
- Participants are not required to stay the full duration of the hackathon (see more in [FAQ](https://cutiehack.com/#faq))

## Judging Criteria
See our [Devpost page](https://cutie-hack-2026.devpost.com) for information regarding judging criteria, what to submit, and more!
