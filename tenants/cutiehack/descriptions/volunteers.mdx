# Cutie Hack 2026 - Volunteer Registration
Welcome to Cutie Hack 2026! Thank you for considering becoming a judge, we appreciate your efforts to help support us this year. Cutie Hack is UC Riverside’s 12 hour, beginner-friendly hackathon hosted by the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) at UCR.

## **Event Information**
**Cutie Hack 2026** takes place throughout **Saturday, November 21, 2026** at **UC Riverside - Winston Chung**.

Volunteer responsibilities include:
- Assisting participants with general inquiries related to logistics
- Setup and clean up for rooms
- Monitoring mini events (workshops/activities) on the day of
- Meal distribution (food handling)

Volunteers are not required to stay the full duration of the event, but are more than welcome to grab free swag (first come, first serve), meals, mini events (workshops/activities), and additional opportunities throughout the day.

Note: volunteers are not permitted to become participants for the hackathon.
