RoseHack 2027 - Judge Registration
Welcome to RoseHack 2027! Thank you for considering becoming a judge. We appreciate your
time and expertise in supporting our hackers.
Event Information
RoseHack 2027 is a 24-hour women-centric hackathon hosted by Women in Computing
(WINC) at UC Riverside, taking place January 30–31, 2027.
Judges are not required to stay the full duration of the event, but are encouraged to check out
the various workshops, speakers, and activities available.
Judge duties include visiting teams to assess their projects on innovation, technical execution,
impact, and presentation, after which they will help determine the winners.
Note: Judges are not permitted to become participants in the hackathon.
