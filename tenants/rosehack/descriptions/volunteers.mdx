RoseHack 2027 - Volunteer Registration
Welcome to RoseHack 2027! Thank you for considering becoming a volunteer. Your support
helps make this event possible.
Event Information
RoseHack 2027 is a 24-hour women-centric hackathon hosted by Women in Computing
(WINC) at UC Riverside, taking place January 30–31, 2027.
Volunteers are not required to stay the full duration of the event, but are encouraged to check
out the various workshops, speakers, and activities available.
Volunteer duties include but are not limited to setup, workshop support, providing assistance to
hackers, food handling, cleanup, and more.
Note: Volunteers are not permitted to become participants in the hackathon.
