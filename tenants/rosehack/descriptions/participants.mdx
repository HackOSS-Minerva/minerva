Welcome to RoseHack 2027! Thank you for your interest in joining us. We're excited to have

Event Information
RoseHack 2027 is a 24-hour women-centric hackathon hosted by Women in Computing
(WINC) at UC Riverside, taking place January 30–31, 2027.
Participants are not required to stay the full duration of the event, but are encouraged to check
out the various workshops, speakers, and networking opportunities available throughout the 24
hours.
