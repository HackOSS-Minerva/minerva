Our Commitment
RoseHack is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment
for all participants. As one of the few women-centric hackathons in California, we are especially
dedicated to ensuring that women and underrepresented groups in tech feel safe, respected,
and empowered throughout the event.
Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Be respectful — Different viewpoints and experiences are welcome.
- Be collaborative — Work together and help others where you can.
- Be inclusive — Welcome new ideas and perspectives, especially from first-time
hackers.
- Be kind — Treat everyone with empathy and consideration.
Unacceptable Behavior
Harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated. This includes:
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability,
appearance, body size, race, or religion.
- Inappropriate language, imagery, or sexual attention.
- Bullying, intimidation, or unwelcome attention.
Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to event organizers
immediately. You can reach us on Discord, email us at ucr.rosehack@gmail.com, or find us at
the event info desk.
Consequences
Unacceptable behavior will not be tolerated. Anyone asked to stop such behavior is expected to
comply immediately. Event organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including
expulsion from the event without warning
