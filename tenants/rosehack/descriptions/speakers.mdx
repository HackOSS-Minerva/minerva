RoseHack 2027 - Speaker Registration
Welcome to RoseHack 2027! Thank you for considering becoming a speaker. We appreciate
your willingness to inspire our participants.
Event Information
RoseHack 2027 is a 24-hour women-centric hackathon hosted by Women in Computing
(WINC) at UC Riverside, taking place January 30–31, 2027.
Speakers will have the opportunity to present their expertise and insights to our participants
during the event. This is a great opportunity to share knowledge and inspire the next generation
of builders, especially women and underrepresented groups in tech.
Note: Speakers are not permitted to become participants in the hackathon.
