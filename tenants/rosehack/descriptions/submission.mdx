RoseHack 2027 - Project Submission
Submit your project for judging. Please fill out your team information and provide links to your
deliverables.
Guidelines
- You must submit a Team Name, Project Name, and Devpost URL.
- You must provide at least one link to GitHub, Figma, or another platform where your
project can be reviewed.
- You may optionally invite other team members to the submission by their email
addresses.
